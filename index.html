Grand Line

The Straw Hat Pirates are an infamous and powerful pirate crew led by Monkey D. Luffy. They sail the treacherous seas in search of the legendary treasure known as the "One Piece" to fulfill their captain's dream of becoming the Pirate King. After eating a mysterious fruit that turns his body into rubber, Luffy sails the dangerous seas to find the ultimate hidden treasure known as the One Piece. He gathers a loyal crew of unique friends who help him chase his grand dream of becoming the King of the Pirates. Together, they battle powerful enemies and challenge a corrupt global government while uncovering the secrets of the world.

Straw Hat Crew

The ten pirates who sail under the flag of the future Pirate King

Monkey D. Luffy

Captain / Rubber Man

The cheerful and fearless captain of the Straw Hat Pirates. After eating the Gomu Gomu no Mi, his body became rubber, granting him incredible elasticity and combat power. His dream is to find the One Piece and become the Pirate King, valuing freedom, friendship, and protecting his crew above all else.

Roronoa Zoro

First Mate / Swordsman

A master of the three-sword style and Luffy's first crewmate. Zoro is a former pirate hunter with an unbreakable will and a promise to become the World's Greatest Swordsman. His loyalty to his captain is absolute, and he takes on the most dangerous enemies with calm precision.

Nami

Navigator / Thief

The brilliant navigator who can read the unpredictable weather of the Grand Line with unmatched skill. Originally a thief, Nami joined Luffy to free her village from Arlong's tyranny. She dreams of drawing a complete map of the world and uses her Clima-Tact to control storms.

Usopp

Sniper / Inventor

The brave yet cowardly sniper of the crew, known for his tall tales and incredible marksmanship. Usopp dreams of becoming a brave warrior of the sea. He wields a slingshot and later develops powerful pop greens, proving his worth in battle despite his fears.

Vinsmoke Sanji

Cook / Martial Artist

The chivalrous cook of the Straw Hats who only uses his legs in combat to protect his hands for cooking. Sanji dreams of finding the All Blue, a legendary sea where all fish from the four oceans meet. He is a master of Black Leg Style and fiercely protective of his crew.

Tony Tony Chopper

Doctor / Zoan Devil Fruit User

A reindeer who ate the Hito Hito no Mi, granting him human intelligence and the ability to transform. Chopper is the crew's doctor and dreams of becoming a panacea capable of curing any disease. Despite his small size, he can become a monstrous fighter with his Rumble Balls.

Nico Robin

Archaeologist / Devil Child

The sole survivor of Ohara and the only person who can read Poneglyphs. Robin joined the Straw Hats after Luffy saved her from the World Government. She dreams of uncovering the true history of the world and finding the Rio Poneglyph, using her Hana Hana no Mi powers.

Franky

Shipwright / Cyborg

A self-made cyborg and the brilliant shipwright who built the Thousand Sunny. Franky dreams of creating a ship that can sail to the end of the Grand Line. He fights with powerful cola-powered weapons and is known for his eccentric "SUPER" personality and loyalty.

Brook

Musician / Skeleton Swordsman

A living skeleton brought back by the Yomi Yomi no Mi. Brook is the crew's musician and a gentleman swordsman who wields a cane sword. He dreams of reuniting with his whale friend Laboon and plays soulful music that can affect the emotions of those who hear it.

Jinbe

Helmsman / Fish-Man Karate Master

A former Warlord of the Sea and captain of the Sun Pirates, Jinbe is a whale shark fish-man who joined the Straw Hats as their helmsman. He is a master of Fish-Man Karate and brings wisdom, honor, and immense experience to the crew, dreaming of true equality between humans and fish-men.
